Privacy Policy

Draft — October 2026

Life After Survivor (“we”) operates the website at lifeaftersurvivor.com. This draft describes, in general terms, how a site like this one typically handles information when someone uses the contact form, the Concierge application, Calendly scheduling, or PayPal checkout.

Information you give us

If you use a form on this site, you choose what to send. Depending on the form, that may include your name, email address, phone number, and the message or application details you type. We use that information to respond to you.

  • The contact form and the Concierge application are submitted through Formspree. Formspree receives the form contents and delivers them to us by email. Formspree’s own privacy policy describes how it handles that data.
  • Booking a call opens Calendly. Calendly collects the scheduling details you enter there. We do not operate Calendly’s systems.
  • Plan payments, when offered, are processed by PayPal. PayPal collects payment information on its own pages. This site does not receive or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use form and scheduling information to answer inquiries, review Concierge applications, and arrange conversations about the program. We do not sell personal information. We do not use this draft to describe advertising profiles, because this static site does not run its own analytics account.

Service providers

The site is hosted as a static site (currently GitHub Pages). Forms are handled by Formspree, scheduling by Calendly, and payments by PayPal. The pages also load fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may process technical data such as IP address and browser details under their own policies when your browser contacts them.

Cookies

This site’s own pages do not set a first-party analytics cookie. Formspree, Calendly, PayPal, and Google Fonts may set or read cookies or similar storage when you use their features. You can control cookies in your browser.

How long information is kept

Messages that reach us are kept long enough to respond and to keep ordinary business records. Formspree, Calendly, and PayPal keep data on their own schedules. This draft does not set a specific retention period; that should be decided with counsel.

Your requests

To ask what we have from a form you sent, or to ask us to delete or correct it, contact us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests. Some records may need to be kept where the law requires it. Requests about data held by Calendly or PayPal may also need to go to those companies.

Children

This site is meant for adults inquiring about a survivorship program. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
